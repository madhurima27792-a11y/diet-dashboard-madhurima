Daily rules

  • Build every meal around protein + fiber + measured complex carbohydrate.
  • Prefer oats, millets, quinoa and controlled portions of roti/rice; avoid sugar, sweets, refined flour and sweet drinks.
  • Use cooked lauki, torai, zucchini, carrots and spinach. Keep raw salads small, especially at night.
  • On vegetarian days, use yellow moong dal tempered with hing + jeera; keep portions steady.
  • Use about 2–3 tsp/day total added oil, favoring mustard/olive oil; include small walnut/flax portions.
  • Hydrate through the day. For bloating, use ginger, ajwain or fennel as tolerated.

Anti-bloating essentials

Prefer
  • Cooked lauki, torai, zucchini
  • Well-cooked carrots/spinach
  • Yellow moong with hing + jeera
  • Ginger, ajwain, fennel
Limit
  • Large raw salads late evening
  • Heavy unfermented dals
  • Fried/processed foods
  • Sugary drinks & desserts

Shopping list

  • Eggs, skinless chicken, plain curd/hung curd
  • Yellow moong dal, oats, quinoa, millet flour/bajra/ragi, whole-wheat atta
  • Lauki, torai, zucchini, carrots, spinach, ginger, coriander
  • Guava/orange/apple/pear; keep fruit to 1 serving at a time
  • Walnuts, flaxseed, pumpkin seeds, makhana
  • Jeera, hing, ajwain, saunf, cinnamon; mustard/olive oil

Substitutions

  • Chicken ↔ 2 eggs + 2 whites or 150 g firm tofu on non-veg days.
  • Quinoa ↔ cooked millet; ½–¾ cup cooked portion.
  • Roti ↔ ½–¾ cup cooked brown rice; pair with protein.
  • Curd ↔ lactose-free curd/unsweetened high-protein yogurt if tolerated.
  • Guava/apple/pear/orange ↔ another whole fruit of similar portion; avoid juice.
For persistent bloating, dairy or any food that repeatedly worsens symptoms should be reduced and discussed with your clinician/dietitian.
