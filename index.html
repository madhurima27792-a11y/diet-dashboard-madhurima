Daily targets & rules

  • Estimated BMR ≈ 1,457 kcal; lightly-active maintenance ≈ 2,004 kcal. A ~1,500 kcal target gives a moderate deficit for roughly 0.5 kg/week.
  • Healthy BMI-range weight at 5'1" is approximately 45–60 kg; use 60 kg as the long-term target and reassess along the way.
  • Pair every carbohydrate with protein and fiber; favor oats, millet, quinoa and measured whole-wheat roti.
  • Avoid added sugar, sweets, sugary drinks, refined flour, fried/ultra-processed foods and trans fats.
  • Favor cooked lauki, torai, zucchini, carrots and spinach; avoid large raw salads and heavy lentils late.
  • Tuesdays and Saturdays are vegetarian: use yellow moong with hing + jeera.
  • Measure mustard/olive oil; use small portions of walnuts/flaxseed. Add ginger, ajwain or fennel as tolerated.

Easy substitutions

  • Chicken → 2 eggs + 2 egg whites on non-veg days.
  • Quinoa → cooked millet or 1 small whole-wheat roti.
  • Curd → lactose-free plain curd if dairy causes bloating.
  • Guava/apple → small orange if better tolerated.
  • Raw salad → cooked lauki, torai, zucchini, carrot or spinach.
