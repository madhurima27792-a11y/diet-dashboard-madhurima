Daily targets & rules

  • Target about 0.5–0.7 kg/week; initial milestone 72 kg, then reassess.
  • Pair carbohydrates with protein and fiber; prefer oats, millet, quinoa and measured whole-wheat roti.
  • Avoid added sugar, sweets, sugary drinks, refined flour and ultra-processed foods.
  • Favor cooked lauki, torai, zucchini, carrots and spinach; avoid large raw salads and heavy lentils late.
  • Tuesdays and Saturdays are vegetarian: use yellow moong with hing + jeera.
  • Measure mustard/olive oil; use small portions of walnuts/flaxseed.
  • Use ginger, ajwain or fennel infusions as tolerated; hydrate through the day.

Easy substitutions

  • Chicken → 2 eggs + 2 egg whites on non-veg days.
  • Quinoa → cooked millet or 1 small whole-wheat roti.
  • Curd → lactose-free plain curd if dairy causes bloating.
  • Guava/apple → small orange if better tolerated.
  • Raw salad → cooked lauki, torai, zucchini, carrot or spinach.
